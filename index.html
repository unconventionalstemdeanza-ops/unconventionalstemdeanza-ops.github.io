Unconventional STEM

Unconventional STEM is a student community at De Anza College for people from diverse and non-traditional backgrounds who share an interest in STEM.

About Us

Unconventional STEM brings together students from different academic and life paths to explore science, technology, engineering, and mathematics. Whether you are returning to school, changing majors or careers, transferring, discovering STEM later, or simply taking a different path, our goal is to create a community where students can connect, collaborate, and explore STEM together.

What We Do

Our activities are shaped by our members' interests and may include collaborative STEM projects, workshops, reading and problem-solving groups, guest speakers, and opportunities to explore research, careers, and different STEM fields. Whether you want to build something, explore a new topic, share an idea, or simply meet other students interested in STEM, there is a place for you here.
